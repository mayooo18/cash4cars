Common Questions

Junk Car FAQ

Do I need a title to sell my car?
In most cases a title makes the process easiest. If you've lost yours or the situation is unusual, give us a call — we can often walk you through the options.
Do you really pick up for free?
Yes. Towing and pickup are always free when we buy your vehicle. The offer you accept is the amount you receive.
What if my car doesn't run at all?
That's fine — most of the vehicles we buy don't start or drive. We bring the equipment to load and haul it away.
How soon can you come get it?
Often the same day, depending on your location and schedule. We'll find a time that works for you.
How do you decide the offer?
The offer is based on the vehicle's year, make, model, condition, and current scrap and parts value. We'll always explain how we got there.
