Our Story

Two Decades on the Road, Right Here at Home

Delta Towing was founded on a simple idea: treat every driver the way you'd want your own family treated on the side of the road. For more than 20 years, we've been the local company that answers the phone at 2 a.m. and actually shows up.

As a family-owned business in Riverton, we know these streets and highways because we drive them every day. That local knowledge means faster response times and operators who genuinely care about doing the job right.

From roadside rescues to buying junk cars, our goal is always the same — honest service, fair prices, and getting you taken care of quickly.
